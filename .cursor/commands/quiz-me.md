# Quiz me

Quiz me on what we just built in this conversation (or in the notebook I mention).

- Ask 3 questions, **one at a time**, and wait for my answer before the next.
- Mix the levels: one about what a piece of code does, one about **why** we made a decision (use `docs/DECISIONS.md`), one "what if" that applies the idea to a slightly different case.
- After each answer: say what was right, correct what was wrong in two or three sentences, and point to the cell or decision that shows it.
- At the end, tell me the one concept I should review, and where in the notebook to look.
