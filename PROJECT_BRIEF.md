# Project brief: Airbnb in Barcelona

## The client

A Barcelona city-council team that is preparing a report on short-term rentals. They are not technical. They have the data but no idea what is worth looking at. They want **you** to:

- find **one question** worth answering,
- answer it **honestly**,
- give them a few **clear charts** they can quote in a meeting.

The question is yours. The agent helps you sharpen it; it does not choose it for you.

The data is described in [`DATASET.md`](DATASET.md).

## Deliverables

1. `eda.ipynb`: your exploration of the data.
2. `NOTES.md`: what you learned about the data, and your question (who it is for, how you measure it).
3. `analysis.ipynb`: runs top to bottom and ends with your answer, written for the client in a few sentences.
4. `docs/DECISIONS.md` and `docs/TECH_STACK.md`, filled in.
